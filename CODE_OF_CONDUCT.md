# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

AuraPro is committed to a professional, respectful, and inclusive project
environment. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

- Communicate constructively and focus discussion on technical substance.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Give and accept actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, publication of private information, sustained disruption, and
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional workplace.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this policy. Repeated or serious violations may
result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the AuraPro-Official organization
contact channels. Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed by
maintainers who are not directly involved in the incident whenever possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever an individual is
representing the project in public or private project-related communication.

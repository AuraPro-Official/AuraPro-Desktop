  Export author: LukeJacob2023
  Original model: NVIDIA, nvidia/stt_ka_fastconformer_hybrid_large_pc
  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/stt_ka_fastconformer_hybrid_large_pc
  Format conversion: NeMo model exported to ONNX by the upstream repository.

Indic ASR default:
  AI4Bharat, ai4bharat/indic-conformer-600m-multilingual (MIT).
  https://huggingface.co/ai4bharat/indic-conformer-600m-multilingual
  Access requires the user's acceptance of the upstream access conditions.
  AuraPro downloads the original ONNX graphs and external tensors unchanged.
  The adapter uses the reference project's CTC path; unused RNNT graphs are
  not downloaded. CPU execution and bounded thread counts are used by default.
